# Batch 02 — Brand Lockups

The lockups in this folder are the approved Zeki Inspector brand compositions.

- `logo-horizontal.png`: horizontal mascot + Zeki Inspector wordmark.
- `logo-vertical.png`: vertical mascot + Zeki Inspector wordmark.

Both files preserve their transparent PNG artwork. Use them directly over the
product's light or dark surfaces; do not add a second background behind the
transparent artwork unless the placement explicitly requires a tile.

The mascot-only mark and compact toolbar icon are maintained in `assets/logo/`
and `assets/extension-icon/` from the approved source artwork in Batch 01.
Do not substitute concept-board crops or alternate mascot generations.
